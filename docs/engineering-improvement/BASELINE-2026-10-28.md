## Known uncertainties

- Rehearsal 5 is incomplete at the cutoff; its final verdict, milestones and CI
  history are unknown here. Its CI could not be read from the observing session.
- The WebAssembly limit's cause is confirmed from runtime source and a reproduction, but no dotnet/runtime documentation or issue describing it was found.
- Rehearsal 4 and Rehearsal 5 share a requirements baseline but differ in
  stack version (R5: echelon-current 1.29.0), team structure and
  remediation history; they are not equivalent workloads.
- No token, cost or tool-call measurement exists for either rehearsal.
- The R5 stream ownership used in the Workstream B replay is reconstructed after
  the fact and is therefore biased toward the observed boundaries.
