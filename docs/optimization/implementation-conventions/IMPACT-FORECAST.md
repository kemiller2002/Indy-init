Rehearsal 010 has not run, so no R10 figure is observed. The machine-readable output is [FORECAST.json](FORECAST.json).

## Model

For each failure mode `f` and each scenario (conservative, central, optimistic):

- **Residual cost.** `residual(f) = P(f occurs in a 420-min build) × critical-path minutes if it occurs × (1 − EXP-INDY-002 overlap)`.
- **Combined prevention.** Conventions that target the same failure mode combine as `1 − Π(1 − prevention)`, so two conventions never prevent the same minute twice.
- **Net saving.** `net(set) = Σ_f residual(f) × combined prevention − Σ competition overhead of the set`.
- **Pre-competition cost is excluded.** It is listed per convention in the catalog, because it is not on the competition's critical path.

How the scenarios are set:
- **Conservative** takes the lowest probability, impact and prevention, the highest overhead, and the largest overlap with EXP-INDY-002.
- **Optimistic** takes the reverse of each.
- **The 305 min measured in R9** sets the optimistic bound for FM-01.
- **The measured 120 min and 90 min WASM losses** bound FM-04.

**Accounting for overlap with EXP-INDY-002.** EXP-INDY-002's parallel, contract-driven strategy targets the same failures as three failure modes:
- FM-08 (shared-file conflicts): 30–50% of its residual cost is assumed already removed by EXP-INDY-002;
- FM-12 (contract revision): 10–30%;
- FM-21 (schedule and gate design): 10–30%.

The conventions are credited only with the remainder. If EXP-INDY-002 removes more of these failure modes, the Tier 2 conventions lose value first.

1. **FM-02 and FM-01 dominate the pool.** These are an ambiguous or changing target, and early stopping or clock misreading. Their impact if they occur is large (60–305 min), but existing checks already enforce both (CV-03, CV-02). Their value is the "secured" line, not new value. Turning those checks off would forfeit roughly that line.
2. **Most of the incremental value comes from CV-11, CV-09, CV-04, CV-17 and CV-10.** These are the real-data contract, durable read-back, WASM boot smoke, current Forma, and typed errors. **The conservative incremental figure is negative:** if recurrence is low, the not-yet-enforced conventions do not pay for their in-competition overhead. Their case rests on the central assumption that a failure family seen in 2–7 earlier rehearsals has a 30–50% chance of recurring.
3. **CV-13 is the clearest case of a rule whose enforcement costs more than it plausibly saves.** It requires a falsifying control for every bound test. It stays negative unless probabilities are raised, which is why it is Tier 3. CV-22 (per-feature file split) is negative at every scale.
4. **The ranking is stable under scaling; the margins are not.**
   - **At half the probabilities:** every Tier 1 convention except CV-19 keeps a positive central net. CV-19 is a safety rule and is enforced regardless. CV-12 and CV-23 fall to about zero.
   - **At 1.5×:** the top-10 membership is unchanged.
5. **The 80% set grows with optimism.** Conservatively, four conventions capture 80% of the (small) peak, and three of them are already enforced. At central about the top 11 are needed, because the pool is spread over many failure modes with small expectations. No single new convention dominates.
